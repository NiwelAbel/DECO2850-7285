# SpyKids — Project Plan (Master Schedule)

**Topic:** Left-behind children (parental absence due to labour migration)
**Course:** DECO2850/DECO7285 Studio 2 — Interaction Design, Sem 2 2026
**Research Question:** How does parental absence affect left-behind children's emotional wellbeing, guidance, and development — and what is actually needed to address this gap?

> **Assessments are a by-product, not the goal.** This schedule is built around doing the project properly — understanding the problem, gathering real evidence, and iterating with care. Assessment 1, Milestones 1–3, Assessment 3, and Assessment 4 all fall out naturally from that work when it's done well. The team's focus should stay on the *work*; the submissions take care of themselves.

**Notes:**

- Assessment 1 is listed under Phase 2 as a by-product, not its own phase — it's an individual deliverable drawn from the shared Phase 1–2 research base, not a gated stage of the project.
- Phase 1 doesn't close until the Problem Identification Document is reviewable and its gate checklist is satisfied — Phase 2 starts from that document.
- Method Team assignments per Week 3 studio outcome. Niwel supports three of the four methods (Literature Review, Interview, Questionnaire/Survey) in addition to authoring the shared documentation — worth keeping an eye on workload balance as Phase 2 progresses.
