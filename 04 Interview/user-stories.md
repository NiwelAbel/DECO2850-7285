# Companion Doll User Stories

These user stories are based on the companion doll project for supporting communication between children and parents who live apart.

## Child User Stories

1. As a child living apart from my parents, I want to record a voice message by pressing one clear button, so that I can easily share moments from my day.

2. As a child, I want to record a short video, so that I can show my parents my drawings, schoolwork, toys, or surroundings.

3. As a child, I want to select a simple emotion or topic label, so that I can express how I feel when I do not know how to begin a conversation.

4. As a child, I want to preview my recording before sending it, so that I can check whether I am comfortable sharing it.

5. As a child, I want to cancel or re-record a message, so that I remain in control of what my parents receive.

6. As a child, I want to save a recording and send it later, so that poor internet access does not prevent me from sharing.

7. As a child, I want the doll to clearly show when its camera or microphone is active, so that I always know when I am being recorded.

8. As a child, I want recording to begin only when I press a button, so that the doll never records me without my knowledge.

9. As a child, I want the doll to notify me gently when my parent replies, so that I know a new message is available without feeling pressured.

10. As a child, I want to choose when to play my parent's reply, so that I can listen when I feel ready and comfortable.

11. As a child, I want to replay my favourite messages, so that I can feel connected to my parents when they are far away.

12. As a child, I want to delete my own recordings and message history, so that I have control over my personal content.

## Parent User Stories

13. As a migrant-worker parent, I want to receive my child's voice and video messages through a mobile app, so that I can understand their daily experiences.

14. As a busy parent, I want to view my child's messages when I have free time, so that different schedules do not prevent us from communicating.

15. As a parent, I want to see the emotion or topic selected by my child, so that I can better understand the context of their message.

16. As a parent, I want to reply with a voice message, so that I can respond warmly and conveniently during a short break.

17. As a parent, I want to reply using text or short video, so that I can choose the most suitable communication method.

18. As a parent, I want editable warm-response templates, so that I can respond thoughtfully when I am unsure what to say.

19. As a busy parent, I want to receive gentle reminders about unanswered messages, so that I do not accidentally forget to reply.

20. As a parent or authorised guardian, I want clear consent and access settings, so that the child's recordings are only available to trusted family members.
